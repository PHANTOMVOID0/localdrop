# Third-party components
Runtime dependencies are downloaded from PyPI during setup and are not vendored.
Their respective licenses and notices apply; inspect the installed distributions
for definitive version-specific license texts.

- FastAPI — https://github.com/fastapi/fastapi (MIT)
- Starlette — https://github.com/encode/starlette (BSD-3-Clause)
- Pydantic — https://github.com/pydantic/pydantic (MIT)
- Uvicorn — https://github.com/encode/uvicorn (BSD-3-Clause)
- aiofiles — https://github.com/Tinche/aiofiles (Apache-2.0)
- python-multipart — https://github.com/Kludex/python-multipart (Apache-2.0)
- python-qrcode — https://github.com/lincolnloop/python-qrcode (BSD)
- Pillow — https://python-pillow.github.io/ (HPND/Pillow license)
- Plyer — https://github.com/kivy/plyer (MIT)
- Optional mkcert — https://github.com/FiloSottile/mkcert (BSD-3-Clause)
- Development: pytest, HTTPX, Playwright; see their installed license notices.

Client HTML/CSS/JavaScript uses system fonts and no external CDN, image, icon,
analytics, or hosted-script dependencies. App icons are locally drawn geometric
arrow artwork, not stock images. mkcert, CA certificates, and browsers are not bundled.
